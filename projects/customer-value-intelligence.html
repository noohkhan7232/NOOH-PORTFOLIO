Project Overview & Business Impact

Customer retention is significantly cheaper than customer acquisition. However, businesses frequently treat all customers identically instead of identifying high-value advocates versus at-risk accounts showing declining purchase activity.

The Platform Solution: Built a Customer Value Intelligence Platform that ingests historical purchase data, calculates Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) metrics, trains machine learning classifiers to predict churn probability, and outputs strategic retention recommendations for marketing teams.

Key Platform Capabilities

  • RFM Segmentation Model: Categorizes customers into "Champions", "Loyal Customers", "At Risk", and "Lost" tiers based on quantitative score cutoffs.
  • Predictive Churn Risk Classifier: Trains Scikit-Learn models on historical purchase gaps to flag customers likely to churn.
  • Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) Calculation: Projects future 12-month net revenue contribution per customer cohort.
  • SQL Analytics Pipeline: Utilizes complex SQL window functions and aggregations to prep clean dataset tables for BI visualization.
